About

Built around your core stability

Core Clinic is a physical therapy and rehabilitation practice designed around one idea: real recovery radiates outward from the center of the body. Every plan begins with a proper assessment, not a generic protocol — so treatment fits the person, not the other way around.

What we treat

Six focus areas that cover most of what brings people through our door — from a fresh injury to a long-standing limitation.

Sports injury rehab

Getting athletes and active people safely back to full performance, not just back on their feet.

Post-surgical recovery

Structured, staged programs that rebuild strength and range of motion after an operation.

Manual & musculoskeletal therapy

Hands-on treatment for joints, muscles and soft tissue, easing pain at its source.

Spinal & postural care

Correcting alignment and movement patterns that put ongoing strain on the back and neck.

Neurological rehabilitation

Rebuilding movement, balance and coordination after a neurological event.

Pain management & mobility

Reducing chronic pain and restoring day-to-day mobility through gradual, guided movement.

Your recovery path

The same four stages, adjusted to every patient — nothing skipped, nothing rushed.

01

Assess

A full picture of the injury, the movement, and the goal — before anything is prescribed.

02

Treat

Hands-on therapy and targeted exercise aimed directly at the root cause, not just the symptom.

03

Strengthen

Progressive loading that rebuilds capacity in the muscles and joints doing the work.

04

Sustain

A home program and follow-up plan so the progress holds long after sessions end.
